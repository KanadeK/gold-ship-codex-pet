# Community conduct

Be specific, respectful, and evidence-led.

Welcome:

- reproducible bug reports
- careful accessibility and safety feedback
- constructive art critique tied to the v2 contract
- translations and documentation corrections
- disagreement that addresses the work rather than the person

Not welcome:

- harassment, sexualization, hate, or threats
- content that violates the Cygames derivative work guidelines
- impersonation or false claims of official affiliation
- posting private data, credentials, or unredacted local paths
- bad-faith disruption or repeated demands after a decision is documented

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and may restrict participation to
protect the project and its community. Report sensitive incidents through the
private channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
